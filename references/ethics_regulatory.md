# Quality, ethics, biosafety, and regulatory gate

## Scope

This is a checklist for identifying review needs, not an approval or legal
opinion. The responsible institution, PI, biosafety officer, veterinarian,
ethics committee, and regulator determine release conditions.

## Quality & Ethics Gate

Record `PASS`, `PASS-WITH-BLOCKERS`, or `BLOCKED` for each applicable area:

- scientific rationale, controls, endpoints, replication, and statistical plan;
- model identity, provenance, authentication, and inclusion/exclusion;
- chemical/biological hazards, exposure routes, engineering controls, PPE,
  decontamination, and waste/disposal;
- biosafety level, containment, recombinant/modified material, and transport;
- animal welfare, 3Rs, analgesia/anesthesia, humane endpoints, monitoring,
  euthanasia, housing, and trained personnel when animals are involved;
- donor/sample consent, privacy, chain of custody, and approvals when human-
  derived material is involved;
- formulation/extract identity, contaminants, sterility/endotoxin, and batch
  traceability;
- equipment calibration, assay QC, data integrity, and deviation handling;
- applicable institutional, jurisdictional, reporting, and regulatory guidance;
  and
- emergency contacts, incident reporting, and stop-work criteria.

Use the current applicable version of each official guideline rather than
copying an outdated requirement into the skill. Record issuer, version/date,
scope, and what it does or does not cover. Examples of guideline families that
may be relevant include animal reporting/welfare guidance, toxicology or
bioanalytical guidance, biosafety rules, and formulation/quality standards; the
agent must verify the actual applicable source for the project.

## Fail-closed safety rule

If a hazard, humane endpoint, approval, containment requirement, or disposal
route is unresolved, block the affected execution. Provide a safe information
request or escalation path; do not improvise operating limits, PPE, doses, or
emergency procedures.
